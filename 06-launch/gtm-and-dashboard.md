## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, The delivery drivers know and have the product but utilization in its current version is low. This feature should drive re-engagement and utilization.
- **Target audience:** The delivery driver is the target audience and as a byproduct coordinators will either use the product or not based on driver engagement.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, A targeted multi-channel approach is need to reach all levels of the company since competition is coming to the market and trying to gain attention. The applications newest revision should address some of the problems that competition has pointed to.

## Channels
1. **Owned: Email to coordinators and leadership informing them of the new product experience, including a short video.**
2. **Earned: Test or limited launch new experience with a trusted customer or two for testimonials that can be posted to social media. Preferably by the customer.**
3. **Owned: In-app first use prompt to drivers notifying them of the experience change and an optional guided walk through.**

## Enablement & Assets
Launch email to coordinators with a short video demo of new experience. An easy way for Sales or Account Management to demo the new experience to customers.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** Ryan from PM owns the plan plus post launch metrics. Tim in Design own the short product video and the guided walk through. Mallory the commercial sales directly will approve and distribute the customer email to the sales team.
- **Timeline:** Phase 1 Week 1-2: Early access to a few customers. Phase 2 Week 2-6: Launch moment, with customer email and commercial engagement. Phase 3 post-launch review metrics on user reengagement to try new feature and continued engagement post first use.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** User re-engagement within the first 2 weeks. Followed up with continued engagement post week 2.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** users don't reengage to try the new experience would likely mean communication has not been successful.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** double-down on engagement efforts
