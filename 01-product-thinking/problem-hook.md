# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic Velocity
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Likely loose customers at an increasing rate and/or fall critically behind in the market.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** If product engagement is down, frontline users are likely using workarounds and thinking about swapping to a competitor.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** The frontline users view the application as burdensome and are disengaging. In turn, leadership can't rely on accurate information.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For the user, we will simplify product layers to essential tasks that surface critical information to the admin. Launching now is critical to re-engage and retain current customers.

- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** It seems to be a systemic problem since it's not just confined to one or two customers.
